The Core Challenge

Raw financial files. Audited metrics.

Corporate financial statements across Indian stock markets are published in fragmented, heterogeneous formats. This capstone project implements an industrial data pipeline that ingests, normalizes, warehouses, and objectively scores every company.

01 / INGESTION

Heterogeneous Data Cleaning

Handles Excel and SQL database dumps with inconsistent column headers, missing fiscal year tags, and non-standard period labels. Regular expressions parse and convert mixed text into standard temporal dimensions.

Relational Star Schema

Designed around dim_company, dim_year, and dim_sector with facts for balance sheets, cash flows, profit and loss, and multi-year CAGR metrics. Supports both local zero-config SQLite and production PostgreSQL.

03 / SCORING

Autonomous 5-Pillar Health Score

Replaces subjective manual analysis with an automated mathematical composite score evaluating profitability, compounding velocity, solvency cushions, and operating cash conversion.

ANALYTICS / ZOMBIE PENALTY
Quantitative Scoring System

A five-pillar mathematical model. No guesswork.

Every company is assigned a score from 0 to 100. Weights reflect essential fundamental health principles: operational profitability, compounding growth, leverage safety, earnings cash quality, and recent performance trajectory.

25%

Profitability

Evaluates Return on Equity (ROE) and Net Profit Margin (NPM). Measures how effectively equity generates net income.

ROE * 2 + NPM * 2
25%

Growth & Velocity

3-Year Compounded Sales and Profit CAGR. Imposes a strict Zombie Penalty if top-line revenue growth is under 5%.

CAGR + Zombie Cap
20%

Solvency Safety

Starts from 100 points. Applies sharp penalties for Debt-to-Equity > 1.0 or 2.0, and Interest Coverage < 3.0 or 1.0.

D/E & IC Cushion
15%

Cash Flow Quality

Validates accounting earnings. Awards bonus points for positive Free Cash Flow and Cash Conversion Ratios exceeding 0.8x.

FCF > 0 / CCR > 0.8
15%

Trend Momentum

Compares Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) net profit against the 3-Year historical average to spot inflection points.

TTM vs 3Y Mean
Classification Score Range Underlying Financial Profile
EXCELLENT 80 to 100 Pristine balance sheet, negligible debt, superior ROE, and robust free cash flow compounding.
GOOD 60 to 79.9 Healthy operating fundamentals with minor capital expenditure or top-line growth deceleration.
AVERAGE 40 to 59.9 Stable business operations with balanced capital allocation and sectoral median efficiency.
WEAK 20 to 39.9 Elevated debt gearing, narrowing margins, or persistent negative free cash flow absorption.
POOR 0 to 19.9 Severe balance sheet stress, persistent operating losses, or critical interest servicing danger.

Dimensional Modeling

Star Schema Data Warehouse. Fast and auditable.

Traditional flat table queries struggle with multi-year aggregations across dozens of financial metrics. The Star Schema model isolates company, calendar year, and sector entities into distinct dimensions while centralizing balance sheets, P&L, and cash flows into clean facts.

DIMENSIONS

Entity & Temporal Reference

dim_company: Ticker, name, sector, NSE/BSE profiles, face value.
dim_year: Fiscal year, quarter, sort order, TTM flag.
dim_sector: Normalized industry groupings.

SURROGATE KEYS / CLEAN JOINS
FACTS / CORE

Financial Statement Warehouse

fact_balance_sheet: Equity, reserves, borrowings, total assets.
fact_profit_loss: Sales, OPM, PBT, net profit, EPS, dividend payout.
fact_cash_flow: Operating, investing, and financing cash flows.

AUDITED MULTI-YEAR ROWS
FACTS / ANALYTICS

Computed Derived Intelligence

fact_analysis: 3Y, 5Y, 10Y Compounded Sales, Profit & ROE.
fact_ml_scores: Autonomous 5-pillar composite health scores.
fact_pros_cons: 540 curated fundamental strengths & risks.

Execution Architecture

Zero Docker friction. Run locally in one click.

While traditional architectures require 5 running Docker containers (PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Beat, Django), this project features a complete Standalone Local Mode powered by SQLite and in-memory caching.

STAGE 01

Ingest Source Data

Parses raw Excel sheets and SQL dumps, verifying schema columns and types.

01_extract_from_mysql.py
STAGE 02

Clean & Derive

Computes debt-to-equity, profit margins, free cash flow, and temporal mappings.

02_clean_and_transform.py
STAGE 03

Load Warehouse

Performs dialect-aware upserts into fact and dimension tables.

03_load_to_warehouse.py
STAGE 04

Score & Serve

Executes scoring algorithms and launches the Django interactive web application.

scoring_engine.py

Quick Start: Standalone Local Mode

Double-click run_without_docker.bat or run_local.bat to run natively on Windows. No Docker Desktop needed.
