What the pipeline solves

Raw Indian equity filings arrive across inconsistent Excel sheets and SQL dumps with varying fiscal-period labels. The ETL pipeline normalizes those records once so downstream scoring and Django views query clean fact and dimension tables.

Ingestion & Cleaning
Parses Excel and MySQL source dumps with regex-based fiscal period normalization, standardizing column names and handling missing values before warehouse load.
Star Schema Storage
Separates dim_company, dim_year, and dim_sector from balance sheet, profit & loss, cash flow, and CAGR fact tables. Runs on SQLite locally or PostgreSQL in production.
Rule-Based Scoring
Applies deterministic weights and explicit penalties (including a low-growth cap when 3-year sales CAGR falls below 5%) instead of opaque black-box scores.

Five-pillar scoring model (0-100)

Each company is scored out of 100 using five weighted components derived from multi-year audited statements.

25% weight

Profitability

Return on Equity (ROE) and Net Profit Margin (NPM) measuring capital efficiency.

ROE * 2 + NPM * 2
25% weight

Growth & Velocity

3-year Sales and Profit CAGR, with a Zombie Penalty cap when top-line growth is below 5%.

3Y CAGR + growth cap
20% weight

Solvency Safety

Starts at 100 and deducts points for Debt-to-Equity above 1.0 or 2.0 and Interest Coverage below 3.0 or 1.0.

D/E & Interest Coverage
15% weight

Cash Flow Quality

Checks whether accounting profit converts into positive Free Cash Flow and Cash Conversion Ratio above 0.8x.

FCF > 0, CCR > 0.8
15% weight

Trend Momentum

Compares Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) net profit against the 3-year historical average.

      Star Schema tables

      Dimensional modeling separates entity metadata from multi-year financial statements and computed analytics tables.

      Dimensions
      dim_company (ticker, NSE/BSE identifiers, sector key), dim_year (fiscal year, quarter, TTM flag), and dim_sector (normalized industry groups).
      Core Facts
      fact_balance_sheet (equity, reserves, borrowings, assets), fact_profit_loss (sales, OPM, PBT, net profit, EPS, dividend payout), and fact_cash_flow (operating, investing, and financing flows).
      Derived Facts
      fact_analysis (3Y, 5Y, 10Y CAGR & ROE), fact_ml_scores (5-pillar health scores), and fact_pros_cons (540 structured strengths and risks).

      Pipeline execution & local mode

      Runs either as a 5-container Docker stack (PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Beat, Django) or in Standalone Local Mode on SQLite without Docker Desktop.

      1. Ingest source dumps etl/01_extract_from_mysql.py Reads Excel sheets and MySQL tables, checking schema columns and data types.
      2. Clean & compute ratios etl/02_clean_and_transform.py Derives Debt-to-Equity, margins, free cash flow, and normalized fiscal dates.
      3. Load Star Schema etl/03_load_to_warehouse.py Upserts dimensions and facts into SQLite or PostgreSQL.
      4. Score & serve analytics/scoring_engine.py Computes 5-pillar scores and serves the Django web interface.
      Standalone Windows launch

      Run run_without_docker.bat or run_local.bat to start the warehouse and Django app locally.
