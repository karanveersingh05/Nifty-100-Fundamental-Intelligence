B100 Fundamental Health Scoring System Methodology

Project Context: Developed by Karan Veer Singh Sikarwar during the Data Analyst Internship at Bluestock Fintech. The scoring engine processes fundamental ratios from the Star Schema data warehouse (PostgreSQL/SQLite) and computes an objective 0 - 100 health composite score for each company.

The 5 Fundamental Pillars & Formulas

Measures capital efficiency and margin pricing power:

  • Return on Equity (ROE): Net Profit divided by Shareholder Equity.
  • Net Profit Margin (NPM): Percentage of revenue converted to net income.
  • Formula: Profitability_Score = Clip((ROE * 2) + (NPM * 2), 0, 100)

Evaluates multi-year top-line and bottom-line velocity:

  • Uses 3-Year Compounded Sales Growth (CAGR) and 3-Year Compounded Profit Growth (CAGR).
  • Zombie Penalty: If Sales CAGR < 5%, growth score is capped at a maximum of 20 to penalize stagnant legacy companies.
  • Formula: Growth_Score = Clip((Sales_CAGR * 3) + (Profit_CAGR * 2), 0, 100)

Assesses balance sheet resilience against credit shocks and rising interest rates:

  • Starts from a clean baseline of 100 points:
  • Debt-to-Equity (D/E): If D/E > 2.0 (-50 pts); If D/E > 1.0 (-25 pts).
  • Interest Coverage (IC): If IC < 1.0 (-50 pts); If IC < 3.0 (-20 pts).
  • Protects investors against overleveraged structures.

Validates accounting earnings against actual liquid cash generation:

  • Baseline score is 50 points:
  • Free Cash Flow (FCF): If FCF > 0, awards +25 points.
  • Cash Conversion Ratio (CCR): Operating Cash Flow / Net Profit. If CCR > 0.8, awards +25 points.

Compares latest Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) Net Profit against the 3-Year historical average:

  • Trend_Ratio = (Latest_Profit - 3Yr_Avg_Profit) / |3Yr_Avg_Profit|
  • Trend_Score = Clip(50 + (Trend_Ratio * 100), 0, 100)
  • Identifies companies on an upward turnaround vs those with decaying earnings.
Classification Thresholds
Label Score Range Financial Implication
EXCELLENT 80 - 100 Negligible debt, high ROE, robust free cash flow, and strong earnings momentum.
GOOD 60 - 79.9 Solid operational performance with minor margin or growth constraints.
AVERAGE 40 - 59.9 Stable business model with balanced risk-reward profile.
WEAK 20 - 39.9 Elevated financial leverage, shrinking margins, or negative free cash flow.
POOR 0 - 19.9 Distressed capital structure, persistent losses, or severe interest repayment stress.
